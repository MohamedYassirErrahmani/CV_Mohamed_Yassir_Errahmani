Profile

Biomedical research — cohorts and regulated studies

EXHES cohort (Exposure and Health Examination Survey)

European HEALS project (FP7-ENV, European Commission) 2017 to present

  • Multicentre prospective mother-child cohort: 2,356 dyads, 10 European countries. Ethics approvals obtained in each country (in France: CPP Ouest IV–Nantes, no. 458/2017).
  • Role: first and corresponding author of the reference publication describing the full cohort protocol (biobank, questionnaires, external/internal exposome) — Methodology (lead), Data curation (equal), Formal analysis (equal), Writing – original draft (lead).
  • Design of the analysis plan, multi-country statistical analyses (meta-analysis of odds ratios and standardised mean differences), drafting and publication of the manuscript.
  • Publication: Errahmani MY et al. The Exposure and Health Examination Survey mother-child(ren) cohort profile. Exposome, 2025. 10.1093/exposome/osaf009

C-HEALTH study

Montpellier University Hospital (CHU) / Institute of Evolutionary Science of Montpellier (ISEM)

  • Research involving human participants (RIPH, French regulatory framework), national no. 2023-A02418-37, sponsored by Montpellier University Hospital. Biological follow-up protocol covering a complete menstrual cycle (daily saliva samples, connected device, blood self-sampling, ovulation tests).
  • Role: lead for the biostatistics component — development of the statistical analysis plan (SAP) and conduct of the analyses.
